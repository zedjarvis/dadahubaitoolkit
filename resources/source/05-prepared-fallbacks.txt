RFQ CHECKLIST
Section 2.1 Mandatory: business registration certificate.
Section 2.2 Mandatory: current tax compliance certificate.
Section 2.3 Mandatory: two office-cleaning references from the past 24 months.
Section 2.4 Mandatory: proposed supervisor's name and role.
Section 2.5 Mandatory: signed quote with routine cleaning, windows, tax and monthly total.
Section 2.6 Mandatory: one-page method statement covering chemicals and site access.
Section 3: monthly budget ceiling KES 120,000 inclusive of applicable taxes; state assumptions, exclusions and clarifications. No prescribed market or tax rate.
Section 4: one PDF; deadline Friday 6 November 2026 at noon EAT. Prepare one PDF for the procurement portal.
Section 4: optional site visit Monday 2 November at 10 AM EAT; not a mandatory bidding condition.
Section 5 explicit rejection grounds: late; missing a mandatory Section 2 item; unsigned quote; over budget.
Section 6 missing: window count, dimensions, height, external high-level access; clarification method/deadline; payment terms; insurance; minimum staffing.
Three useful clarifications: full window scope and safe access; payment terms; how and when to submit clarification questions.
Do not describe unspecified items as mandatory.

If notebook citations fail, open the original text and inspect these sections directly. This reference does not simulate clickable model citations.
