Before running the prompt ask: "Which item do you think earns most in total?"

Top gross-profit contributors: cooking oil, rice, toilet paper.
Slowest by sell-through (sold / opening): soap 17.5%; tea and noodles tied at 20%.
Days of remaining stock at the historical daily rate: toilet paper 2.9;
flour 4.1; oil 4.3; rice 7.9. Lead times could change the ordering priority.
Teaching point: tea has a higher gross margin percentage than oil, but oil
contributes more total gross profit. Name the metric before asking for a ranking.

Assumptions: no restocking, returns, shrinkage, stockouts, promotions or price
changes; constant demand; immediate delivery; no safety stock; all amounts on a
consistent simplified tax basis. Reorder = max(0, units sold - closing units).
This is a classroom top-up calculation, not a recommendation for a real shop.
Check two different rows, the formulas and the totals. One correct row does
not validate the rest of an AI-generated table.
