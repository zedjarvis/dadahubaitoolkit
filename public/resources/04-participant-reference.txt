START WITH THE TASK
Client replies, proposals and summaries: ChatGPT.
Questions about a document, with a source to inspect: Gemini Notebook
(formerly NotebookLM; your account may still use the familiar name).
Sales and stock patterns: ChatGPT, with independently checked calculations.
A small calculator or other prototype: Codex; give it the goal, rules and
checks, then review the delivered files and working result before business use.
Repetitive steps across apps: explore automation after your process is clear.

A USEFUL BRIEF
Role: Who should help me?
Context: What facts and constraints do they need?
Task: What exactly should they do?
Format: What should the result look like?
Check: What must be verified, and what must not be invented?

REUSABLE PROMPT
Act as [role]. I run [business]. Use only these facts: [facts].
Help me [specific task]. Return [format and length].
Keep [important constraints]. Do not invent prices, dates or commitments.
List missing information separately and show me what I should check.

THREE CHECKS
1. Does the output preserve the facts I gave it?
2. Can I verify the important claim, calculation or source citation?
3. What is still missing before I use this in my business?

FOR THE WORKSHOP
Use the supplied fictional examples. Do not paste real student records,
private client messages or other people's personal information into the demo.
For real work, use data you are authorised to process and the appropriate
organisational tools and controls. Paying for a plan alone does not settle that.
