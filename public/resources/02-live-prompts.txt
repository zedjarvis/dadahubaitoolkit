owner. Use a calculation/data-analysis tool if available and tell me if it is
not available. Treat the following as a simplified classroom example.

Assume no restocking, returns, shrinkage, price changes or stockouts during the

Calculate per product:
1. Revenue = units sold × selling price.
2. Gross profit = units sold × (selling price - unit cost).
3. Closing stock = opening stock - units sold.
4. Sell-through percentage = units sold / opening stock × 100.
5. Top-up quantity = max(0, units sold - closing stock).

Give one table and the total revenue, cost of goods sold and gross profit.
Rank the top three products by total gross-profit contribution.
Define slow-moving as lowest sell-through percentage and name the bottom
three, noting ties. Explain the next actions in no more than five bullets.
Show the formulas and the worked calculation for rice and cooking oil.
State that overheads are absent, so net profit cannot be determined. State
which real-world information is missing before a purchasing decision.

Business rules for this fictional training example:
- Floor area in square metres must be a finite number greater than zero.
- Visits per week: choose 1, 3 or 5.
- Windows: yes or no.
- Use exactly 52 / 12 weeks per month to estimate average monthly visits.
- Cleaning price: KES 18 per square metre per visit.
- Windows: a fixed KES 6,000 per month when selected.
- Subtotal = area × 18 × visits per week × 52 / 12 + windows fee.
- Apply an illustrative 16% VAT to the entire subtotal, including windows.
- Total = subtotal + illustrative VAT.
- Retain full precision during calculation; display currency to two decimals.

Show area, average visits per month, cleaning amount, windows amount, subtotal,
illustrative VAT and grand total, all clearly labelled. Explain that 52/12 is
an average estimate; actual calendar visits may differ. Label the rates and

matches our fictional RFQ; do not claim it is a final commercial quote.
For invalid or missing area input, show a helpful error and no valid quote.
Use large text, clear form labels and a professional design.
