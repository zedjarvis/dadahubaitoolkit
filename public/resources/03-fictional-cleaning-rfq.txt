SECTION 1 — PURPOSE AND SCOPE
The fictional Parklands Learning Centre invites quotations for routine office
cleaning at its fictional Parklands premises. The cleaned floor area is 400
square metres. Service is required three times per week: Monday, Wednesday
and Friday after 6:00 PM. The proposed start date is 16 November 2026. The
initial contract term is three months.
Routine cleaning covers floors, desks, washrooms and waste collection to the
designated on-site collection point. The contractor supplies cleaning products,
equipment and staff. Window cleaning once per month is included in the requested
scope and must be priced separately. The number, size and height of windows,
and the need for external high-level access, have not yet been specified.

SECTION 2 — MANDATORY SUBMISSION ITEMS
A submission must include all of the following:
2.1 A copy of the bidder's business registration certificate.
2.2 A current tax compliance certificate.
2.3 Two references for office-cleaning work completed in the last 24 months.
2.4 The name and role of the proposed site supervisor.
2.5 A signed quotation showing the monthly routine-cleaning amount, monthly
window-cleaning amount, any applicable taxes and the monthly total.
2.6 A one-page method statement explaining safe chemical use and how cleaning
staff will follow the client's site-access procedures.

The budget ceiling is KES 120,000 per month inclusive of all applicable taxes.
The bidder must state assumptions, exclusions and any required clarifications.
This document does not prescribe a per-square-metre market rate or a tax rate.
No contract award is promised by this request.

SECTION 4 — SUBMISSION AND SITE VISIT
